Setting the Standard

At Carpenthree, every piece leaves our workshop to a standard we would want in our own homes. We cut on modern CNC machines and finish with traditional joinery. The result is carpentry built for Singapore's humidity and for everyday family life.

Best Quality

Free islandwide delivery and installation on furniture orders over S$1,000. That includes HDB lift-landing assessments.
