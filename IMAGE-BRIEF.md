# Carpenthree: Image Brief

The site currently shows **stock placeholder photos** as inspiration. Each one has a small badge with the filename to replace.
Save your own photo under that exact name in `images/` and it replaces the placeholder automatically.

**Overall look:** warm and natural wood tones against greige, charcoal and off-white. Use soft daylight or warm 3000K lighting. Keep compositions uncluttered and editorial, with plenty of negative space. Avoid busy backgrounds and harsh flash.

## Home page
| File | Shape | What to shoot |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Wide landscape, 2400×1400 | Dramatic, moody shot of your workshop or a finished interior. Darker tones work best because white text sits on top. |
| `intro-chair.jpg` | Portrait 4:5, 1200×1500 | Your signature piece (chair, stool or side table) on a plain dark backdrop, studio-style. |
| `project-wardrobe.jpg` | Landscape 4:3 | A finished built-in wardrobe in an HDB or condo bedroom, doors partly open to show the interior fittings. |
| `project-kitchen.jpg` | Landscape 4:3 | A finished kitchen: cabinets, countertop and pendant lights. Shoot it straight-on and symmetrical. |
| `craftsman.jpg` | Square or portrait | A team member at work: hands, tools, a candid smile. It should feel genuine. |
| `bathroom.jpg` | Portrait | A vanity cabinet with a basin and mirror, with timber against stone or tiles. |
| `wwd-1.jpg` | Portrait | A timber feature wall, fluted panels or a TV console detail. |
| `wwd-2.jpg` | Portrait | A full room view of a completed project showing several carpentry pieces. |
| `workshop-1.jpg` | Wide landscape | Workshop close-up: wood shavings, a hand plane, sawdust, raw timber. |
| `workshop-2.jpg` | Square | Showroom or workshop interior with material samples on display. |
| `workshop-3.jpg` | Square | A kitchen or dining area from a completed project. |
| `workshop-4.jpg` | Wide landscape | A living room from a completed project, with the TV console and shelving in view. |

## Shop
| File | Shape | What to shoot |
|---|---|---|
| `shop-hero.jpg` | Wide landscape, 2400×1000 | A styled room set featuring several of your furniture pieces. |
| `products/<product-id>.jpg` | Square, 1200×1200 | Each product on a **consistent** plain light-greige backdrop, at the same angle (about 3/4 view) and the same lighting, so the grid looks uniform. |

Product IDs: `teak-dining-table`, `oak-dining-chair`, `walnut-bench`, `low-sideboard`, `coffee-table`, `tv-console`, `bed-frame`, `bedside-table`, `study-desk`, `bookshelf`, `shoe-cabinet`, `wardrobe`.

## Tips
- Export as JPG, around 200–400 KB each. Compress them at squoosh.app.
- Use exactly the same filename, in lowercase.
- When all your photos are in, turn the placeholders off by setting `enabled: false` in `js/placeholders.js`.
- Stock placeholders come from Unsplash and LoremFlickr. They're for inspiration only, so replace them before launch.
